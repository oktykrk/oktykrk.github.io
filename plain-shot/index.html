A FEW PLAIN ANSWERS

Good to know.

Do my screenshots ever leave my Mac?

PlainShot does not upload your screenshots. Capture, annotation, and history stay on your Mac. If you save to a synced folder, use Universal Clipboard, or share a file yourself, those services follow your settings. Read the privacy policy.

Do I need an account or an internet connection?

There’s no PlainShot account. Capturing and editing run locally. Apple handles trial activation, purchases, and restoring purchases through the App Store; those actions may need an internet connection and your Apple Account.

Does it record video or audio?

No. PlainShot is for still screenshots. macOS requires Screen Recording permission to capture the screen, but PlainShot does not record video or audio. It does not ask for Accessibility or Input Monitoring permission.

How does the free trial work?

Start the 30-day trial in Settings → Access. It never charges automatically. After the trial, the optional one-time Permanent Unlock enables new captures. Your existing history remains accessible. App Store availability and pricing will be announced at launch.

Can I edit a screenshot later?

Annotations can be adjusted while your capture is open. Completed captures are flattened PNGs; local history does not store editable layers. You can view, copy, save, or move a history item to Trash.
