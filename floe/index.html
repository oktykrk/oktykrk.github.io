A few things to know

Before you
find your flow.

What does my Mac need?

Floe requires macOS 15 or later and supports Apple silicon and Intel Macs. Some apps restrict how their windows can be resized. Full-screen windows and windows in other Spaces have additional macOS limitations.

Why does Floe ask for Accessibility access?

macOS requires your permission before Floe can move and resize other apps’ windows. Floe observes window and mouse activity for window management; it does not record the text you type.

Do I need Screen Recording permission?

Only for optional window thumbnails. Floe captures still previews, not a continuous recording. You can turn thumbnails off and continue arranging windows.

How will the trial and license work?

The 7-day trial will start when you first launch Floe. After the trial, a one-time license will enable window management on up to two active Macs. Purchases will use Lemon Squeezy, and you’ll activate Floe with the license key from your receipt.

Is Floe on the Mac App Store?

Floe is being prepared for direct download, signed with Developer ID and notarized by Apple. The download will be linked here when the release is available.

What about privacy?

Your layouts and preferences stay on your Mac. Licensing contacts Lemon Squeezy, and Floe sends automatic crash and hang diagnostics through Sentry. Read the privacy policy for the full details.
