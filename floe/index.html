Will it work on my Mac?+

Floe requires macOS 15 or later and works on Apple silicon and Intel Macs. Some apps limit how their windows can be resized. Full-screen windows and windows in other Spaces have additional macOS limitations.

Why does Floe need Accessibility access?+

macOS requires your permission before Floe can move and resize other apps’ windows. Floe observes window and mouse activity for window management; it does not record the text you type.

What about Screen Recording permission?+

It’s only used for optional window thumbnails in Snap Assist. Floe captures still previews, not a continuous recording. You can turn thumbnails off and keep arranging windows.

How does the free trial work?+

The 7-day trial starts when you first launch Floe. No payment is required. Purchasing and license activation are not available yet. When the trial ends, window management pauses and Settings remains accessible. Reinstalling doesn’t reset the trial. Once purchasing opens, a planned $9.99 USD one-time license will cover two active Macs.

Is Floe on the Mac App Store?+

Floe is a direct download, signed with Developer ID and notarized by Apple. Open the DMG and drag Floe into Applications. It isn’t distributed through the Mac App Store.

Does my window content stay on my Mac?+

Yes. Window content, previews, layouts, and preferences stay on your Mac. Licensing uses Lemon Squeezy, update checks use GitHub, and automatic crash and hang diagnostics go through Sentry. The privacy policy explains the details.
