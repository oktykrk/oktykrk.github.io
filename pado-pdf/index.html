Pado PDF · iPhone & iPad

Everyday PDF tools.
On your device.

Merge documents, split a PDF, extract the pages you need, and compress files before sharing. Pado PDF also converts PDF pages to images and creates PDFs from photos. Your documents are processed on your iPhone or iPad, without a Pado account.

Merge, split, and organize PDF pages

Combine PDFs into one document or split a file into separate parts. Select pages by tapping or entering ranges, then reorder, duplicate, remove, or rotate them. Preview the result before you share it or save a copy to Files.

Compress PDFs and convert images

Reduce a PDF's size for sharing, export pages as JPEG or PNG images, or turn photos into a PDF. Crop and rotate photos, then choose the page size and margins. Compression results depend on the source document; a requested target size may not be achievable.

Free tools and optional Pado Pro

Every tool is available to try for free, with operation limits explained in the app. Pado Pro removes specified commercial limits and adds quality and target-size options. Choose a monthly or yearly subscription, or a one-time lifetime purchase. Current prices and billing terms appear in the App Store before purchase. Device and processing safety limits apply to every plan.

Your documents stay under your control

PDF processing happens on your device. Pado PDF works on imported copies and leaves your original selected documents unchanged. Save important results to Files or another destination; deleting the app removes files stored only inside it. Purchases and restoration use online services, and file providers such as iCloud follow your own settings. See the privacy policy for details.

Requirements and support

Pado PDF requires iOS 17 or iPadOS 17 or later. It supports English, Turkish, German, Spanish, and French. If a file will not import, compression does not reach your target, or a purchase needs restoring, visit Pado PDF Support for troubleshooting and contact details.
